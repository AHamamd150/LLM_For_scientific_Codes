[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase does not specify whether "leading b-jet" refers to the highest-pT b-tagged jet among all selected jets, or the highest-pT b-tagged jet among the jets that passed all previous selection cuts.

Question to user:
Are the leading b-jet and subleading b-jet defined before or after applying all selection cuts?

Golden axe:
The leading b-jet is defined among the selected b-tagged jets that passed all previous cuts.

Silver axe:
The leading b-jet is defined among all b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of previous cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
