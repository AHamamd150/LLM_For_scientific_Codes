# Physics Process: p p > t t~, t > b W+, W+ > l+ nu, t~ > b~ W-, W- > j j
# ========================================================================

[SELECTION_CUTS]
- Select electrons with pT > 25 GeV and |eta| < 2.5
- Select muons with pT > 25 GeV and |eta| < 2.4
- Require exactly one lepton (electron or muon)
- Require at least 4 jets with pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5
- Among these selected jets, require at least 2 b-tagged jets.
- Among the selected b-tagged jets, define the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R.
- Select MET > 20 GeV
- Select transverse mass of the lepton and MET between 30 and 150 GeV (W candidate)
- Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected non-b-tagged jets.
- Select the hadronic W mass between 40 to 120 GeV.
- Build the hadronic top candidate from the leading selected b-tagged jet and the hadronic W candidate.
- Select the hadronic top mass between 100 to 250 GeV.

Apply the above cuts sequentially and skip the event immediately when a cut is not satisfied.

[PLOTS_FOR_VALIDATION]
- Plot the following as histograms:
1 Plot the transverse mass of lepton + MET (W leptonic candidate)
2 Plot the MET distribution
3 Plot the pT of the leading b-jet
4 Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet
5 Plot the invariant mass of the W hadronic candidate in range 40 to 120 GeV
6 Plot the invariant mass of the hadronic top candidate in range 100 to 250 GeV
7 Plot delta R between the leading lepton and the closest selected  b-jet
8 Plot the HT distribution (scalar sum of all jet pT)
9 Plot the number of jets per event
10 Plot eta of the leading b-jet


Use simple validation histograms.
For each histogram: plt.figure(); plt.hist(values, bins=40, density=True); plt.xlabel(...); plt.ylabel("density"); plt.savefig(...); plt.close().
Use concrete short ASCII labels and filenames.

[OUTPUT_STRUCTURE]
- After the event loop, print one cutflow summary with the number of events after each selection step.
- Save the plots in png format
