Original phrase:
"Build z_obj from the two selected electrons using summed four-momentum. Define the selected muon as the W candidate lepton."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "selected muon" may refer to the first muon in the event (raw event-level object) or the only muon that passed the selection cuts. This ambiguity affects the definition of the W candidate lepton and the subsequent cuts and plots.

Question to user:
Are the selected muon and selected electrons defined as the only objects that passed the respective cuts, or are they the first objects in the event that meet the criteria?

Golden axe:
The selected muon is the only muon that passed the selection cuts.

Silver axe:
The selected muon is the first muon in the event that meets the criteria, regardless of other muons in the event.

Golden axe
