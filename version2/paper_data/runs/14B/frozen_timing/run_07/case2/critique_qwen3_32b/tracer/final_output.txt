SECTION 2: DEFINITION SUMMARY

* [CHECK CAREFULLY] DEFINITION SUMMARY

* selected_electrons
  - computed/defined as: [e for e in electrons if e['pt'] > 25 and abs(e['eta']) < 2.5]
  - upstream chain: electrons -> get_electrons(event)
  - collection definition: e['pt'] > 25 and abs(e['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent

* selected_muons
  - computed/defined as: [m for m in muons if m['pt'] > 25 and abs(m['eta']) < 2.4]
  - upstream chain: muons -> get_muons(event)
  - collection definition: m['pt'] > 25 and abs(m['eta']) < 2.4
  - structure: single-constituent

* selected_leptons
  - computed/defined as: selected_electrons + selected_muons
  - upstream chain: selected_electrons, selected_muons
  - collection definition: e['pt'] > 25 and abs(e['eta']) < 2.5 for electrons; m['pt'] > 25 and abs(m['eta']) < 2.4 for muons
  - structure: multi-input

* selected_jets
  - computed/defined as: [j for j in jets if j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5]
  - upstream chain: jets -> get_jets(event)
  - collection definition: j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent

* selected_bjets
  - computed/defined as: [b for b in bjets if b['pt'] > 30 and abs(b['eta']) < 2.5]
  - upstream chain: bjets -> get_bjets(event)
  - collection definition: b['pt'] > 30 and abs(b['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent

* met
  - computed/defined as: helper_get_met(event)
  - upstream chain: event['objects']
  - collection definition: o['type'] == 6
  - structure: single-constituent

* mt
  - computed/defined as: helper_transverse_mass(lepton, met)
  - upstream chain: lepton (selected_leptons[0]), met
  - collection definition: lepton from selected_leptons, MET from event
  - structure: composite-derived

* w_hadronic
  - computed/defined as: sum_objects(jet1, jet2)
  - upstream chain: jet1, jet2 (from selected_light_jets)
  - collection definition: jet1 and jet2 from selected_light_jets
  - structure: composite-derived

* w_mass
  - computed/defined as: helper_invariant_mass(jet1, jet2)
  - upstream chain: jet1, jet2 (from selected_light_jets)
  - collection definition: jet1 and jet2 from selected_light_jets
  - structure: composite-derived

* top_hadronic
  - computed/defined as: sum_objects(bjet1, w_hadronic)
  - upstream chain: bjet1 (from selected_bjets), w_hadronic
  - collection definition: bjet1 from selected_bjets, w_hadronic from jet1 and jet2
  - structure: composite-derived

* top_mass
  - computed/defined as: helper_invariant_mass(bjet1, w_hadronic)
  - upstream chain: bjet1 (from selected_bjets), w_hadronic
  - collection definition: bjet1 from selected_bjets, w_hadronic from jet1 and jet2
  - structure: composite-derived

* dr_lepton_bjet
  - computed/defined as: helper_delta_r(lepton, bjet)
  - upstream chain: lepton (selected_leptons[0]), bjet (from selected_bjets)
  - collection definition: lepton from selected_leptons, bjet from selected_bjets
  - structure: composite-derived

* ht
  - computed/defined as: scalar_pt_sum(selected_jets)
  - upstream chain: selected_jets
  - collection definition: j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent

* njet
  - computed/defined as: len(selected_jets)
  - upstream chain: selected_jets
  - collection definition: j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent
