[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading jets in selection vs. validation

Original phrase:
"Select invariant mass of the two leading jets between 60 and 100 GeV (W hadronic candidate)"
and
"Plot the invariant mass of the two leading jets (W hadronic) in the range 40 to 120 GeV"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "two leading jets" may refer to the two highest-pT jets in the full jet collection (before any cuts), or to the two highest-pT jets among the selected jets (after applying pT and eta cuts). This distinction affects which jets are used in the invariant mass calculation and validation plots.

Question to user:
Are the "two leading jets" defined before or after applying the jet pT and eta cuts?

Golden axe:
The two leading jets are selected after applying the jet pT and eta cuts.

Silver axe:
The two leading jets are selected from the full jet collection before any cuts are applied.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[AMBIGUITY 2] Two-jet system in delta phi calculation

Original phrase:
"Plot delta phi between the lepton and the two-jet system"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "two-jet system" could refer to a reconstructed composite object formed by combining the two leading jets, or it could refer to the scalar quantity derived from the two jets (e.g., their combined transverse momentum or invariant mass). This distinction affects how the delta phi is calculated.

Question to user:
Is the "two-jet system" a reconstructed composite object formed by combining the two leading jets, or is it a scalar quantity derived from them?

Golden axe:
The two-jet system is a reconstructed composite object formed by combining the two leading jets.

Silver axe:
The two-jet system refers to a scalar quantity derived from the two leading jets, such as their combined transverse momentum.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[AMBIGUITY 3] Delta phi between lepton and MET

Original phrase:
"Plot delta phi between the lepton and MET"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "delta phi between the lepton and MET" could be interpreted as the azimuthal angle difference between the lepton and the MET vector, or it could be interpreted as the azimuthal angle difference between the lepton and the MET direction. These are mathematically equivalent, but the phrasing may lead to different implementations if the helper function is not explicitly called.

Question to user:
Should the delta phi be calculated between the lepton and the MET vector, or between the lepton and the MET direction?

Golden axe:
The delta phi is calculated between the lepton and the MET vector.

Silver axe:
The delta phi is calculated between the lepton and the MET direction.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
