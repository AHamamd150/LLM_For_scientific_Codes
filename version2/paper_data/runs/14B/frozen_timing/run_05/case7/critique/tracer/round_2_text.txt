

* delta R between taus
  - computed/defined as: helper_delta_r(leading tau, subleading tau)
  - upstream chain: leading tau, subleading tau
  - collection definition: math.sqrt(deta^2 + dphi^2)
  - structure: multi-input

* delta phi between ditau_obj and dijet_obj
  - computed/defined as: helper_delta_phi(ditau_obj['phi'], dijet_obj['phi'])
  - upstream chain: ditau_obj, dijet_obj
  - collection definition: normalized Δφ calculation
  - structure: multi-input

* centrality of ditau system relative to jets
  - computed/defined as: centrality_between_jets(ditau_obj, leading jet, subleading jet)
  - upstream chain: ditau_obj, leading jet, subleading jet
  - collection definition: |eta_obj - eta_center_jj| / |delta_eta_jj|
  - structure: multi-input

* cutflow
  - computed/defined as: dictionary with keys and values tracking event counts
  - upstream chain: cutflow
  - collection definition: initial events, after each selection step
  - structure: single-constituent
