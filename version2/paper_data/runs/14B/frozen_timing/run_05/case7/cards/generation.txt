# Physics Process: p p > H j j, H > ta+ ta-
# =========================================

[SELECTION_CUTS]
- Select taus with pT > 20 GeV and |eta| < 2.3
- Select jets with pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 4.7
- Require at least 2 taus
- Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus.
- Define ditau_obj from the summed four-momentum of the leading tau and subleading tau. 
- Require at least 2 jets (VBF topology)
- Define the leading jet and subleading jet from the selected jets.
- Define dijet_obj from the summed four-momentum of the leading jet and subleading jet.
- Require opposite-sign tau pair
- Select invariant mass of the leading and subleading jets (m_jj) > 400 GeV
- Select |delta eta| between the two leading jets > 2.5
- Select ditau invariant mass between 60 and 150 GeV
- Veto events with b-tagged jets among the selected jets 

Apply the above cuts sequentially and skip the event immediately when a cut is not satisfied.
Increment each cutflow counter immediately after its cut is passed.

[PLOTS_FOR_VALIDATION]
- Plot the following as histograms:
1 Plot the ditau invariant mass in the range 50 to 160 GeV
2 Plot the dijet invariant mass (m_jj) in the range 400 to 2000 GeV
3 Plot delta eta between the two leading jets
4 Plot the pT of the leading tau
5 Plot the pT of the subleading tau
6 Plot the pT of the leading jet
7 Plot the pT of the subleading jet
8 Plot delta R between the two taus
9 Plot delta phi between the ditau_obj and the dijet_obj
10 Plot the centrality of the ditau system relative to the two jets

Use simple validation histograms.
For each histogram: plt.figure(); plt.hist(values, bins=40, density=True); plt.xlabel(...); plt.ylabel("density"); plt.savefig(...); plt.close().
Use concrete short ASCII labels and filenames.


[OUTPUT_STRUCTURE]
- After the event loop, print one cutflow summary with the number of events after each selection step.
- Save the plots in png format
