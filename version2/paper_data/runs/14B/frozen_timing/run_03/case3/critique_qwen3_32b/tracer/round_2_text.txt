

* eta_subleading_photon_list
  - computed/defined as: subleading_photon['eta']
  - upstream chain: subleading_photon
  - collection definition: pseudorapidity of subleading photon
  - structure: single-constituent

* dr_photons_list
  - computed/defined as: helper_delta_r(leading_photon, subleading_photon)
  - upstream chain: leading_photon, subleading_photon
  - collection definition: sqrt((eta1 - eta2)^2 + (delta_phi(phi1, phi2))^2)
  - structure: multi-input

* dphi_photons_list
  - computed/defined as: helper_delta_phi(leading_photon['phi'], subleading_photon['phi'])
  - upstream chain: leading_photon['phi'], subleading_photon['phi']
  - collection definition: normalized to [-π, π]
  - structure: multi-input

* cos_theta_star_list
  - computed/defined as: abs(cos_theta_star_collins_soper(leading_photon, subleading_photon))
  - upstream chain: leading_photon, subleading_photon
  - collection definition: Collins-Soper frame calculation
  - structure: composite-derived

* cutflow
  - computed/defined as: dictionary with counters for each selection step
  - upstream chain: cutflow["Initial"], cutflow["Photon selection"], etc.
  - collection definition: incremented after each selection step
  - structure: multi-input
