[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading jets in selection vs. validation

Original phrase:
"Select invariant mass of the two leading jets between 60 and 100 GeV (W hadronic candidate)"
and
"Plot the invariant mass of the two leading jets (W hadronic) in the range 40 to 120 GeV"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "two leading jets" could refer to the two highest-pT jets in the full jet collection (before any selection cuts) or the two highest-pT jets among the selected jets (after applying pT and eta cuts). This distinction affects which jets are used for the invariant mass calculation and validation.

Question to user:
Are the "two leading jets" defined before or after applying the jet pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5 cuts?

Golden axe:
The two leading jets are defined after applying the jet pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5 cuts.

Silver axe:
The two leading jets are defined before applying the jet pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5 cuts.

Recommended default:
