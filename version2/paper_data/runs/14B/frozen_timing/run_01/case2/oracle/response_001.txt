[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "leading b-jet" could refer to the highest-pT b-tagged jet before or after selection cuts. This affects which jet is selected for the plot.

Question to user:
Are the leading b-jet and subleading b-jet defined before or after applying the selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Leading b-jet is defined after applying the selection cuts.

Silver axe:
Leading b-jet is defined before applying the selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
