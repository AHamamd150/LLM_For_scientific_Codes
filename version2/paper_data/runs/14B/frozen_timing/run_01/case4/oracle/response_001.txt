[AMBIGUITY 1] Selection of z_obj and W candidate lepton

Original phrase:
"Build z_obj from the two selected electrons using summed four-momentum. Define the selected muon as the W candidate lepton."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "selected muon" could refer to the first muon in the event (raw event-level object) or the first muon that passes the selection cuts (selected object). This distinction affects whether the W candidate lepton is defined before or after applying the pT > 20 GeV cut.

Question to user:
Are the selected muon and selected electrons defined as the first muon/electrons in the event, or as the first muon/electrons that pass the pT and eta cuts?

Golden axe:
The selected muon is defined as the first muon in the event that passes the pT > 10 GeV and |eta| < 2.4 cuts.

Silver axe:
The selected muon is defined as the first muon in the event, regardless of whether it passes the pT > 10 GeV and |eta| < 2.4 cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[AMBIGUITY 2] Definition of three-lepton system

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the three-lepton system built from the two selected electrons and the selected muon"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "three-lepton system" could be interpreted as a reconstructed composite object (sum of four-momenta) or as a scalar quantity derived from the three leptons. This affects whether the pT is computed as the vector sum of the three leptons or as a scalar quantity.

Question to user:
Is the three-lepton system defined as a reconstructed composite object (sum of four-momenta) or as a scalar quantity derived from the three leptons?

Golden axe:
The three-lepton system is defined as a reconstructed composite object built from the four-momenta of the two selected electrons and the selected muon.

Silver axe:
The three-lepton system refers to a scalar quantity derived from the three leptons, not a reconstructed composite object.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[AMBIGUITY 3] Definition of transverse mass of W candidate lepton + MET

Original phrase:
"Select transverse mass of W candidate lepton + MET > 30 GeV"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "W candidate lepton + MET" could be interpreted as the transverse mass of the W candidate lepton and MET, or as the transverse mass of the system formed by the W candidate lepton and MET. This affects whether the transverse mass is computed from the W lepton and MET individually or as a composite system.

Question to user:
Is the transverse mass computed from the W candidate lepton and MET individually, or from the system formed by the W candidate lepton and MET?

Golden axe:
The transverse mass is computed from the W candidate lepton and MET individually.

Silver axe:
The transverse mass is computed from the system formed by the W candidate lepton and MET.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
