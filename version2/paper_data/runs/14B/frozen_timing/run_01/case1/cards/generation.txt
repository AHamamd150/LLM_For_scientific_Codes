# Physics Process: p p > W+ W-, W+ > l+ nu, W- > j j
# ================================================================

[SELECTION_CUTS]	
- Select electrons with pT > 25 GeV and |eta| < 2.5
- Select muons with pT > 20 GeV and |eta| < 2.4
- Use selected electrons and selected muons to define selected leptons.
- Require exactly 1 selected lepton
- Require at least 2 jets with pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5
- Select MET > 30 GeV
- Veto events with b-tagged jets 
- Select transverse mass of lepton + MET between 40 and 120 GeV (W leptonic candidate)
- Select invariant mass of the two leading jets between 60 and 100 GeV (W hadronic candidate)
- Select delta R between the two leading jets < 3.0

Apply the above cuts sequentially and skip the event immediately when a cut is not satisfied.

[PLOTS_FOR_VALIDATION]
- Plot the following as histograms:
1 Plot the transverse mass of lepton + MET (W leptonic) in the range 30 to 150 GeV
2 Plot the invariant mass of the two leading jets (W hadronic) in the range 40 to 120 GeV
3 Plot the MET distribution
4 Plot the pT of the lepton
5 Plot the pT of the leading jet
6 Plot the pT of the subleading jet
7 Plot delta R between the two leading jets
8 Plot delta phi between the lepton and MET
9 Plot the eta of the lepton
10 Plot delta phi between the lepton and the two-jet system

Use simple validation histograms.
For each histogram: plt.figure(); plt.hist(values, bins=40, density=True); plt.xlabel(...); plt.ylabel("density"); plt.savefig(...); plt.close().
Use concrete short ASCII labels and filenames.


[OUTPUT_STRUCTURE]
- After the event loop, print one cutflow summary with the number of events after each selection step.
- Save the plots in png format
- Save the following in a CSV file for MLP analysis:
  1- Transverse mass of lepton + MET
  2- Dijet invariant mass
  3- MET
  4- pT of the lepton
  5- Delta R between the two jets
  6- Delta phi between lepton and the two-jet system
