[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase does not specify whether "leading b-jet" refers to the highest-pT b-tagged jet among all selected jets, or the highest-pT b-tagged jet among the jets selected after all cuts.

Question to user:
Are the "leading b-jet" and "subleading b-jet" plots based on the b-tagged jets selected after all cuts, or on the full b-tagged jet collection before any cuts?

Golden axe:
The leading b-jet refers to the highest-pT b-tagged jet among the selected jets after all cuts.

Silver axe:
The leading b-jet refers to the highest-pT b-tagged jet among all b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
