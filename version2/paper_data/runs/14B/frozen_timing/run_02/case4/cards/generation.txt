# Physics Process: p p > W Z, W > l nu, Z > l+ l-
# ================================================

[SELECTION_CUTS]
- Select electrons with pT > 15 GeV and |eta| < 2.5.
- Select muons with pT > 10 GeV and |eta| < 2.4.
- Require exactly 2 selected electrons and exactly 1 selected muon.
- Require the two selected electrons to have opposite charge.
- Build z_obj from the two selected electrons using summed four-momentum.
- Select invariant mass of z_obj between 76 and 106 GeV.
- Define the selected muon as the W candidate lepton.
- Require MET > 30 GeV.
- The W candidate muon  must have pT > 20 GeV.
- Select transverse mass of W candidate muon + MET > 30 GeV.

Apply the above cuts sequentially and skip the event immediately when a cut is not satisfied.
Increment each cutflow counter immediately after its cut is passed.

[PLOTS_FOR_VALIDATION]
- Plot the following as histograms:
1 Plot the invariant mass of z_obj in range 60 to 120 GeV
2 Plot the transverse mass of the W candidate lepton + MET
3 Plot the MET distribution
4 Plot the pT of the three-lepton system built from the two selected electrons and the selected muon
5 Plot the pT of z_obj
6 Plot the pT of the W candidate muon 
7 Plot delta phi between z_obj and MET
8 Plot the invariant mass of the three-lepton system
9 Plot delta R between the two electrons forming z_obj
10 Plot eta of the W candidate muon 

Use simple validation histograms.
For each histogram: plt.figure(); plt.hist(values, bins=40, density=True); plt.xlabel(...); plt.ylabel("density"); plt.savefig(...); plt.close().
Use concrete short ASCII labels and filenames.


[OUTPUT_STRUCTURE]
- After the event loop, print one cutflow summary with the number of events after each selection step.
- Save the plots in png format
