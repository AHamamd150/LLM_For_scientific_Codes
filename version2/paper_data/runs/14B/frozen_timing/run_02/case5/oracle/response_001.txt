[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading object definitions

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase does not clarify whether the leading and subleading taus are defined before or after applying the "opposite-sign tau pair" cut. This affects which objects are considered for the ditau system and subsequent cuts.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading taus defined before or after applying the opposite-sign requirement?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined after the opposite-sign requirement is applied.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined before the opposite-sign requirement is applied.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
