[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "leading b-jet" could refer to the highest-pT b-tagged jet in the full event or the highest-pT b-tagged jet among those selected after the jet selection cuts.

Question to user:
Are the leading b-jet and subleading b-jet in the plots defined before or after the jet selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Leading b-jet is defined among the selected b-tagged jets after the jet selection cuts.

Silver axe:
Leading b-jet is defined among all b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected non-b-tagged jets."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "selected non-b-tagged jets" could refer to jets that passed the jet selection cuts but were not b-tagged, or it could refer to all non-b-tagged jets in the event regardless of selection cuts.

Question to user:
Are the non-b-tagged jets used to build the hadronic W candidate required to have passed the jet selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Non-b-tagged jets used for the hadronic W candidate must have passed the jet selection cuts.

Silver axe:
Non-b-tagged jets used for the hadronic W candidate can be any non-b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
