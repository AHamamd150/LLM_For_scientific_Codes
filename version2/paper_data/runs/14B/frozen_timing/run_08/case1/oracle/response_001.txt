[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading jets in selection vs. validation

Original phrase:
"Select invariant mass of the two leading jets between 60 and 100 GeV (W hadronic candidate)"
and
"Plot the invariant mass of the two leading jets (W hadronic) in the range 40 to 120 GeV"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "two leading jets" may refer to jets selected after cuts or to the raw jet collection. This affects whether the invariant mass is computed from the same jet pair in both selection and validation.

Question to user:
Are the "two leading jets" in the selection and validation steps defined using the same jet collection (after all jet cuts) or from the full jet collection before cuts?

Golden axe:
The "two leading jets" refer to the two highest-pT jets after all jet cuts have been applied.

Silver axe:
The "two leading jets" refer to the two highest-pT jets in the full jet collection before any cuts are applied.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
