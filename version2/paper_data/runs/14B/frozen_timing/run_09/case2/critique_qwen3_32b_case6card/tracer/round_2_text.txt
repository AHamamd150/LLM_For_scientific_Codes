

* top_candidate

  - upstream chain: leading_bjet = helper_get_leading(selected_bjets), w_hadronic = sum_objects(leading_light_jet, subleading_light_jet)
  - collection definition: selected_bjets = [b for b in bjets if b['pt'] > 30 and abs(b['eta']) < 2.5], selected_light_jets = [j for j in light_jets if j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5]
  - structure: composite-derived

* dr_lepton_bjet

  - collection definition: selected_leptons = selected_electrons + selected_muons, selected_bjets = [b for b in bjets if b['pt'] > 30 and abs(b['eta']) < 2.5]
