[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "leading b-jet" could refer to the highest-pT b-tagged jet in the full event or the highest-pT b-tagged jet among those selected after the jet selection cuts.

Question to user:
Are the leading b-jet and subleading b-jet plots based on the b-tagged jets selected after the jet selection cuts, or on all b-tagged jets in the event?

Golden axe:
The leading b-jet refers to the highest-pT b-tagged jet among those selected after the jet selection cuts.

Silver axe:
The leading b-jet refers to the highest-pT b-tagged jet in the full event, regardless of selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
