[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading jets in selection vs. validation

Original phrase:
"Select invariant mass of the two leading jets between 60 and 100 GeV (W hadronic candidate)"
and
"Plot the invariant mass of the two leading jets (W hadronic) in the range 40 to 120 GeV"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "two leading jets" could refer to the two highest-pT jets in the full jet collection or the two highest-pT jets among the selected jets after cuts. This distinction affects which jets are used for the invariant mass calculation and validation.

Question to user:
Are the "two leading jets" in the invariant mass selection and validation based on the full jet collection or only the selected jets after all cuts?

Golden axe:
The two leading jets are selected from the jets that pass the pT and eta cuts.

Silver axe:
The two leading jets are selected from the full jet collection before any cuts.

Golden axe

Original phrase:
"Plot delta phi between the lepton and the two-jet system"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "two-jet system" could refer to a reconstructed composite object formed by summing the four-momenta of the two leading jets, or it could refer to a scalar quantity derived from the two jets (e.g., their combined transverse momentum or invariant mass). This distinction affects how the delta phi is computed.
