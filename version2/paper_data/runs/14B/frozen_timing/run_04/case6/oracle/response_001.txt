[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "leading b-jet" could refer to the highest-pT b-tagged jet in the full event or the highest-pT b-tagged jet among those selected after the jet and b-tagging cuts.

Question to user:
Are the leading b-jet and subleading b-jet defined before or after the jet and b-tagging selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Highest-pT b-tagged jet among those selected after the jet and b-tagging cuts.

Silver axe:
Highest-pT b-tagged jet in the full event, regardless of selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected non-b-tagged jets."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "selected non-b-tagged jets" could refer to jets that passed the jet selection cuts but were not b-tagged, or it could refer to jets that were not selected as b-tagged jets regardless of their selection status.

Question to user:
Are the non-b-tagged jets used in the hadronic W candidate required to have passed the jet selection cuts (pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5)?

Golden axe:
Jets that passed the jet selection cuts and were not b-tagged.

Silver axe:

Golden axe
