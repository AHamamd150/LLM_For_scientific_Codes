[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet" and "Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The leading and subleading b-jets could be defined either before or after the b-jet selection cuts. This affects which jets are considered in the ordering.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading b-jets be determined from all b-tagged jets in the event, or only from those that pass all previous selection criteria?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are defined among the selected b-tagged jets that pass all prior cuts.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are defined among all b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Plot the pT of the leading selected b-jet" and "Plot the pT of the subleading selected b-jet"
- Silver axe rewrite: "Plot the pT of the leading b-jet in the event" and "Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet in the event"


[AMBIGUITY 2] Hadronic W candidate definition

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected non-b-tagged jets."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "selected non-b-tagged jets" could refer to jets that have passed all prior selection criteria or only to jets that passed the basic jet selection (pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5).

Question to user:
Should the hadronic W candidate be built from the two leading jets that passed all previous selection cuts, or only from the jets that passed the basic jet selection criteria?

Golden axe:
The hadronic W candidate is built from the two leading jets that passed all previous selection cuts.

Silver axe:
The hadronic W candidate is built from the two leading jets that passed only the basic jet selection criteria (pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5).

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected non-b-tagged jets that passed all previous cuts"
- Silver axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets with pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5"

The closest b-jet is determined from the selected b-tagged jets that passed all previous selection criteria.

Golden axe
