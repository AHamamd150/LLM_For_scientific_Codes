[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R"

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the "selected b-jet" refers to the full b-jet collection before any cuts or only the subset of b-jets that passed all previous selection criteria.

Question to user:
Should the "closest b-jet" be determined from all b-tagged jets in the event, or only from those that passed all previous selection cuts?

Golden axe:
The closest b-jet should be determined from the full b-jet collection before any selection cuts.

Silver axe:
The closest b-jet should be determined from the subset of b-jets that passed all previous selection criteria.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R, among all b-tagged jets"
- Silver axe rewrite: "the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R, among the selected b-tagged jets"
