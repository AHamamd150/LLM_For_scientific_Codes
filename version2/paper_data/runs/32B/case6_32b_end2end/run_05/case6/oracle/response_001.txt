[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Among the selected b-tagged jets, define the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether "leading b-jet" refers to the b-jet with highest pT among selected b-jets, or the b-jet with smallest delta R to the selected lepton. These are different physical objects that would require different helper functions.

Question to user:
Are you defining the "leading b-jet" as (1) the highest-pT b-tagged jet, or (2) the b-tagged jet with smallest delta R to the selected lepton?

Golden axe:
The leading b-jet is the one with highest transverse momentum among selected b-tagged jets.

Silver axe:
The leading b-jet is the one with smallest angular separation (delta R) to the selected lepton.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading b-jet as the highest-pT b-tagged jet among selected b-jets"
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading b-jet as the b-tagged jet with smallest delta R to the selected lepton"
