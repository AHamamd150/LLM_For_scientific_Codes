[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet" and "Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "leading b-jet" and "subleading b-jet" could be interpreted as the highest and second-highest pT b-jets in the full jet collection (including non-b-tagged jets), or as the highest and second-highest pT b-jets only among the selected b-tagged jets.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading b-jets be determined from all jets in the event (including non-b-tagged jets), or only from the selected b-tagged jets?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are the highest and second-highest pT b-tagged jets among the selected b-tagged jets.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are the highest and second-highest pT b-tagged jets in the full jet collection, regardless of selection.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Plot the pT of the leading b-jet among the selected b-tagged jets" and "Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet among the selected b-tagged jets"
- Silver axe rewrite: "Plot the pT of the leading b-jet in the full jet collection" and "Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet in the full jet collection"

"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected non-b-tagged jets."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "leading selected non-b-tagged jets" could be interpreted as the two highest-pT jets in the full jet collection that are not b-tagged, or as the two highest-pT jets in the selected jet collection (after pT and eta cuts) that are not b-tagged.

Silver axe:

Recommended default:
Golden axe

- Silver axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets in the full jet collection"
