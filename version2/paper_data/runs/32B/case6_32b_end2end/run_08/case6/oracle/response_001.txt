[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet" and "Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The selection requires at least 2 b-tagged jets, but the phrase "leading b-jet" could refer to the highest-pT b-jet among all jets in the event, or only among the selected b-tagged jets. This affects which jets are considered in the pT ranking.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading b-jets be defined based on all jets in the event, or only among the selected b-tagged jets?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are defined among the selected b-tagged jets.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are defined among all jets in the event, regardless of b-tagging.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Plot the pT of the leading b-jet among selected b-tagged jets"
- Silver axe rewrite: "Plot the pT of the leading b-jet in the full jet collection"
