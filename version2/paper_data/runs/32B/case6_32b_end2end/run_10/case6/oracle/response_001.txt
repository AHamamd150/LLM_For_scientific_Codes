[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Among the selected b-tagged jets, define the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether "closest in delta R" refers to the b-jet with the smallest delta R to the lepton among all selected b-tagged jets, or if it refers to a previously defined "leading" b-jet in pT. The phrase could be interpreted as either a new selection or a property of an already defined object.

Question to user:
Do you want to define a new b-jet as the one with the smallest delta R to the lepton, or are you referring to a property of the leading (highest-pT) b-jet?

Golden axe:
Define a new b-jet as the one with the smallest delta R to the selected lepton.

Silver axe:
Compute delta R between the already defined leading b-jet and the selected lepton.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

- Silver axe rewrite: "Compute the delta R between the selected lepton and the leading b-jet."
