

* HT (scalar sum of jet pT)
  - computed/defined as: scalar_pt_sum(jets)
  - upstream chain: [j for j in get_jets(event) if j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5]
  - collection definition: j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent

* Number of jets
  - computed/defined as: len(jets)
  - upstream chain: [j for j in get_jets(event) if j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5]
  - collection definition: j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent

* Leading b-jet pT
  - computed/defined as: bjets_sorted[0]['pt']
  - upstream chain: sort_by_pt(bjets) -> bjets_sorted

  - structure: single-constituent

* Subleading b-jet pT
  - computed/defined as: bjets_sorted[1]['pt']
  - upstream chain: sort_by_pt(bjets) -> bjets_sorted
  - collection definition: b['pt'] > 30 and abs(b['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent

* Leading b-jet eta

  - structure: single-constituent

* Delta R between lepton and closest b-jet
  - computed/defined as: helper_delta_r(lepton, closest_bjet)
  - upstream chain: helper_delta_phi(lepton['phi'], closest_bjet['phi'])
  - collection definition: lepton and closest_bjet
  - structure: composite-derived
