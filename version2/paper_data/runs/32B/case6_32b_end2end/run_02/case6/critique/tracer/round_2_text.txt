

Subleading b-jet pT
  - computed/defined as: bjet2_pt
  - upstream chain: bjets_sorted[1]['pt']
  - collection definition: bjets with b['pt'] > 30 and abs(b['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent

* Hadronic W mass values
  - computed/defined as: w_had_mass
  - upstream chain: helper_invariant_mass(light_jets_sorted[0], light_jets_sorted[1])
  - collection definition: two leading non-b-tagged jets
  - structure: composite-derived

* Hadronic top mass values
  - computed/defined as: top_had_mass
  - upstream chain: helper_invariant_mass(bjets_sorted[0], w_had)
  - collection definition: leading b-jet and hadronic W candidate
  - structure: composite-derived

* Delta R between lepton and closest b-jet
  - computed/defined as: dr_lep_bjet
  - upstream chain: helper_delta_r(lepton, closest_bjet)
  - collection definition: lepton and closest b-jet
  - structure: composite-derived

* HT values
  - computed/defined as: ht_values
  - upstream chain: scalar_pt_sum(jets)
  - collection definition: jets with j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: composite-derived

* Number of jets per event
  - computed/defined as: njets_values
  - upstream chain: len(jets)
  - collection definition: jets with j['pt'] > 30 and abs(j['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent

* Leading b-jet eta
  - computed/defined as: bjet1_eta
  - upstream chain: bjets_sorted[0]['eta']
  - collection definition: bjets with b['pt'] > 30 and abs(b['eta']) < 2.5
  - structure: single-constituent
