[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet" and "Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "leading b-jet" could refer to the highest-pT b-tagged jet among all jets in the event (raw collection), or only among the selected b-tagged jets after earlier cuts. This affects which jets are considered and how they are ordered.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading b-jets be determined from all b-tagged jets in the event, or only from those that passed all previous selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are defined from the selected b-tagged jets after all prior cuts.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are defined from all b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of selection status.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Plot the pT of the leading b-jet among selected b-tagged jets"
- Silver axe rewrite: "Plot the pT of the leading b-jet in the full event"


[AMBIGUITY 2] Hadronic W candidate construction

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected non-b-tagged jets"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "leading selected non-b-tagged jets" could mean the two highest-pT jets in the full jet collection that are non-b-tagged and selected, or the two highest-pT jets in the non-b-tagged subset of selected jets. This affects which jets are used for the W candidate.

Question to user:
Should the hadronic W candidate be built from the two highest-pT jets in the full selected jet collection that are non-b-tagged, or from the two highest-pT jets in the selected non-b-tagged jet subset?

Golden axe:
Hadronic W candidate is built from the two highest-pT jets in the selected non-b-tagged jet subset.

Silver axe:
Hadronic W candidate is built from the two highest-pT jets in the full selected jet collection that happen to be non-b-tagged.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading jets in the selected non-b-tagged jet collection"
- Silver axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected jets that are non-b-tagged"


[AMBIGUITY 3] Delta R between lepton and b-jet

Original phrase:
"Plot delta R between the leading lepton and the closest selected b-jet"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "closest selected b-jet" could mean the b-tagged jet with the smallest delta R to the lepton among all selected jets, or among all b-tagged jets in the event. This affects which jet is used for the delta R calculation.

Question to user:
Should the closest selected b-jet be determined only among selected b-tagged jets, or among all b-tagged jets in the event?

Golden axe:
Closest selected b-jet is determined only among selected b-tagged jets.

Silver axe:
Closest selected b-jet is determined among all b-tagged jets in the event.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Plot delta R between the leading lepton and the closest b-jet among selected b-tagged jets"
- Silver axe rewrite: "Plot delta R between the leading lepton and the closest selected jet that is b-tagged"
