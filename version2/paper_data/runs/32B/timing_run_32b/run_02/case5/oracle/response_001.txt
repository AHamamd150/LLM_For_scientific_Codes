[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading object definitions

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase does not specify whether the leading and subleading taus are defined before or after applying the pT and |eta| selection cuts. This can lead to different implementations depending on whether the ordering is applied to the raw collection or the selected collection.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading taus determined from the full collection of taus in the event, or only from the subset of taus that pass the pT and |eta| selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined from the selected taus (after applying pT and |eta| cuts).

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined from the raw collection of taus in the event, before applying any selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus (after applying pT and |eta| cuts)."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the full collection of taus in the event, before applying any selection cuts."

[AMBIGUITY 2] Dijet system definition

Original phrase:
"Define dijet_obj from the summed momentum of the leading jet and subleading jet."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the dijet system should be treated as a reconstructed composite object (with its own properties like mass and direction) or if it's just a scalar quantity derived from the two jets. This affects how the system is used in subsequent calculations.

Question to user:
Should the dijet system be treated as a reconstructed composite object with its own four-momentum, or is it only needed for calculating scalar quantities like invariant mass and delta eta?

Golden axe:
The dijet system should be treated as a reconstructed composite object with its own four-momentum.

Silver axe:
The dijet system is only needed for calculating scalar quantities like invariant mass and delta eta.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:

- Silver axe rewrite: "Calculate the dijet system properties (like invariant mass and delta eta) directly from the leading and subleading jets without forming a composite object."
