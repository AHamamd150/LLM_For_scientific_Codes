[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading objects in selected collections

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the leading and subleading objects are defined before or after the full selection is applied. If the selection includes cuts on the tau properties, the definition of "leading" and "subleading" could change depending on whether the ordering is done before or after all cuts.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading taus defined based on the raw tau collection before any cuts, or based on the selected tau collection after all cuts?

Leading and subleading taus are defined from the selected taus after all cuts.

Golden axe

- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the raw tau collection before any cuts."
