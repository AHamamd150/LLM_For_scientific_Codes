[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading objects defined before or after cuts

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "selected taus" implies that the selection cuts have already been applied, but the exact timing of when "selected taus" is defined is not clear. If the leading and subleading objects are defined before applying all cuts, it could result in different objects being used in later validation plots and final output compared to the case where they are defined after all cuts.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading taus defined after all tau selection cuts (pT > 20 GeV and |eta| < 2.3) have been applied?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined after applying the tau selection cuts.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined before applying the tau selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the taus that pass the pT > 20 GeV and |eta| < 2.3 cuts."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from all taus in the event before applying the pT > 20 GeV and |eta| < 2.3 cuts."
