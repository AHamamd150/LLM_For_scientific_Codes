[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading object definition timing

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the leading and subleading objects are defined before or after applying all selection cuts. This can lead to different implementations depending on whether the ordering is done on the raw collection or the final selected objects.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading tau be determined from the full tau collection before any selection, or from the tau collection after all selection cuts have been applied?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading tau are defined from the selected taus after all cuts are applied.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading tau are defined from the full tau collection before any selection is applied.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus after all selection cuts."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the full tau collection before applying any selection cuts."
