[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading tau definition timing

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
This could be interpreted as either (1) selecting taus first and then defining leading/subleading among them, or (2) defining leading/subleading before applying any selection cuts. The timing of this definition affects which objects are used in subsequent steps like the ditau system construction.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading taus be defined after applying the pT and eta cuts, or before any selection is applied?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined from the selected taus (after pT and |eta| cuts).

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined from the raw tau collection before any selection is applied.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus (after pT and |eta| cuts)."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the raw tau collection before applying any selection cuts."

[AMBIGUITY 2] Dijet system definition scope

Original phrase:
"Define dijet_obj from the summed momentum of the leading jet and subleading jet."

Why this is dangerous:
This could be interpreted as either (1) creating a composite object representing the dijet system for later use in angular variables, or (2) only using the summed momentum for the m_jj invariant mass calculation without creating a persistent object. This affects how the dijet system is referenced in the delta phi and centrality calculations.

Question to user:
Should the dijet system be defined as a persistent composite object for use in multiple calculations, or is it only needed for the m_jj invariant mass calculation?

Golden axe:
Define a persistent composite dijet object for use in multiple calculations.

Silver axe:
Only calculate the summed momentum for the m_jj invariant mass without creating a persistent object.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define dijet_obj as a composite object from the summed four-momentum of the leading jet and subleading jet for use in multiple calculations."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Calculate the summed momentum of the leading jet and subleading jet only for the m_jj invariant mass calculation."

[AMBIGUITY 3] B-jet veto scope

Original phrase:
"Veto events with b-tagged jets"

Why this is dangerous:
This could be interpreted as either (1) vetoing events with any b-tagged jet in the full event, or (2) vetoing events with b-tagged jets among the selected jets (after pT and |eta| cuts). The scope of the veto affects how b-tagged jets are counted and whether jets failing the pT/eta cuts are considered.

Question to user:
Should the b-jet veto apply to all b-tagged jets in the event regardless of their pT and |eta|, or only to b-tagged jets that pass the jet selection criteria?

Golden axe:
Veto events with any b-tagged jet in the full event, regardless of pT and |eta|.

Silver axe:
Veto events only if there are b-tagged jets among the selected jets (after pT and |eta| cuts).

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Veto events with any b-tagged jet in the full event, regardless of pT and |eta|."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Veto events if there are b-tagged jets among the selected jets (after pT and |eta| cuts)."
