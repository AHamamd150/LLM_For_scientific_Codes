[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading tau definition timing

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the leading and subleading tau are defined before or after all selection cuts (including opposite-sign requirement and invariant mass cuts). This affects which tau pair is used for the ditau system and subsequent cuts.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading tau be defined based on the raw selected tau collection (after pT and |eta| cuts), or should the definition wait until after all selection criteria including opposite-sign and invariant mass cuts?

Golden axe:
Define leading and subleading tau from the selected tau collection after pT and |eta| cuts but before other selection criteria.

Silver axe:
Define leading and subleading tau only after all selection criteria including opposite-sign and invariant mass cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus (after pT and |eta| cuts) for use in subsequent cuts."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus only after all selection criteria including opposite-sign and invariant mass cuts."

Golden axe:
Treat the dijet system as a full reconstructed composite object with all four-momentum properties.

Silver axe:

Recommended default:
Golden axe

- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the dijet system as the scalar quantity derived from the leading jet and subleading jet."

[AMBIGUITY 3] Jet collection for b-tag veto

Original phrase:
"Veto events with b-tagged jets"

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the b-tag veto applies to all jets in the event or only to the selected jets that passed the pT and |eta| cuts. This affects the jet collection used for the veto.

Question to user:
Should the b-tag veto apply to all jets in the event, or only to the jets that passed the pT and |eta| selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Apply the b-tag veto only to jets that passed the pT and |eta| selection cuts.

Silver axe:
Apply the b-tag veto to all jets in the event regardless of selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Veto events with b-tagged jets among the selected jets (after pT and |eta| cuts)."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Veto events with any b-tagged jets in the full event, regardless of selection cuts."
