[AMBIGUITY 1] Definition of "leading tau and subleading tau"

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the leading and subleading taus are defined before or after the "opposite-sign tau pair" cut. If defined before, the opposite-sign requirement may discard the originally leading/subleading pair, requiring a reselection. If defined after, the pair is fixed once the cut is applied.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading taus be defined before or after the opposite-sign requirement is applied?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined after the opposite-sign requirement is applied.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined from the selected taus before the opposite-sign requirement is applied.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus after requiring an opposite-sign pair."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus before applying the opposite-sign requirement."
