[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading object definition timing

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the leading/subleading objects are defined before or after all selection cuts are applied. The timing of this definition affects which objects are considered in subsequent steps like the ditau system construction.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading tau be defined from the selected taus at this point in the sequence, or should they be re-evaluated after all selection cuts are applied?

Golden axe:
Define the leading and subleading tau from the selected taus at this point in the sequence.

Silver axe:
Define the leading and subleading tau from the selected taus only after all selection cuts are applied.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus at this point in the sequence."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus only after all selection cuts are applied."

Original phrase:
"Veto events with b-tagged jets"

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the veto applies to all jets in the event or only to the selected jets that passed the pT and eta cuts. This affects how b-jets are counted and whether unselected jets can trigger the veto.

Question to user:
Should the b-jet veto apply to all jets in the event, or only to the jets that passed the pT and eta selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Apply the b-jet veto only to jets that passed the pT and eta selection cuts.

Silver axe:
Apply the b-jet veto to all jets in the event regardless of selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
