[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading object definitions

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."
"Define the leading jet and subleading jet from the selected jets."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase is ambiguous about whether the leading/subleading objects are defined before or after applying the pT and |eta| cuts. This affects which objects are considered in the ordering.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading objects defined from the full collection before cuts, or from the collection after applying the pT and |eta| cuts?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading objects are defined from the collection after applying the pT and |eta| cuts.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading objects are defined from the full collection before applying any cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus after applying the pT and |eta| cuts."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from all taus in the event before applying any cuts."


[AMBIGUITY 2] Composite system definition

Original phrase:
"Define ditau_obj from the summed four-momentum of the leading tau and subleading tau."
"Define dijet_obj from the summed four-momentum of the leading jet and subleading jet."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase is ambiguous about whether this is a reconstructed composite object (with all kinematic properties) or just a scalar quantity derived from the sum.

Question to user:
Should the ditau and dijet objects be treated as full reconstructed composite objects with all kinematic properties, or is the sum only needed for specific observables like invariant mass?

Golden axe:
The ditau and dijet objects should be treated as full reconstructed composite objects with all kinematic properties.

Silver axe:
Only the summed four-momentum is needed for observables like invariant mass, and no full composite object needs to be reconstructed.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define a reconstructed ditau system from the leading and subleading tau."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Compute the summed four-momentum of the leading and subleading tau for observables like invariant mass."


[AMBIGUITY 3] Jet veto scope

Original phrase:
"Veto events with b-tagged jets among the selected jets"

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase is ambiguous about whether the veto applies to all jets in the event or only to the selected jets that passed the pT and |eta| cuts.

Question to user:
Should the b-tag veto be applied to all jets in the event, or only to the jets that passed the pT and |eta| selection cuts?

Golden axe:
The veto should apply only to the jets that passed the pT and |eta| selection cuts.

Silver axe:
The veto should apply to all jets in the event regardless of whether they passed the selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Veto events with b-tagged jets among the selected jets after applying the pT and |eta| cuts."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Veto events with any b-tagged jets in the entire event, regardless of selection cuts."
