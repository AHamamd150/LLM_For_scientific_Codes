[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading and subleading object definitions

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase could be interpreted as either using the highest and second-highest pT taus in the raw collection or in the selected collection. The selection cuts may change the pT ordering.

Question to user:
Should the leading and subleading taus be determined before or after applying the pT and |eta| selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading taus are selected from the raw collection before applying any cuts.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading taus are selected from the collection after applying the pT and |eta| cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the raw tau collection before applying any cuts."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected tau collection after applying the pT and |eta| cuts."

[AMBIGUITY 2] Dijet system definition

Original phrase:
"Define dijet_obj from the summed momentum of the leading jet and subleading jet."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the dijet system should be defined as a reconstructed composite object with four-momentum, or if it's just a reference to the scalar quantities derived from the two jets.

Question to user:
Should the dijet system be defined as a reconstructed composite object with four-momentum, or is it sufficient to refer to the two jets separately for all calculations?

Golden axe:
Define a reconstructed composite object with the summed four-momentum of the two jets.

Silver axe:
Use the two jets separately for all calculations without defining a composite object.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Define a dijet composite object from the summed four-momentum of the leading jet and subleading jet."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Use the leading jet and subleading jet separately for all calculations without defining a composite system."

[AMBIGUITY 3] B-jet veto scope

Original phrase:
"Veto events with b-tagged jets"

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether the veto applies to all jets in the event or only to the selected jets that passed the pT and |eta| cuts.

Question to user:
Should the b-tagged jet veto be applied to all jets in the event or only to the jets that passed the pT and |eta| selection cuts?

Golden axe:
Apply the veto to all jets in the event regardless of selection cuts.

Silver axe:
Apply the veto only to jets that passed the pT and |eta| selection cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Veto events with any b-tagged jets in the full event."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Veto events with b-tagged jets among the selected jets passing pT and |eta| cuts."
