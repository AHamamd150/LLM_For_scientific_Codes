[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic top candidate from the leading b-jet and the hadronic W candidate."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether "leading b-jet" refers to the highest-pT b-jet among all jets in the event, or the highest-pT b-jet among selected b-jets after all previous cuts.

Question to user:
Are you defining "leading b-jet" as the highest-pT b-jet in the full event, or only among the selected b-jets that passed the b-tagging and pT/eta cuts?

Golden axe:
Leading b-jet is the highest-pT b-jet among selected b-jets after all cuts.

Silver axe:
Leading b-jet is the highest-pT b-jet in the full event, regardless of selection status.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic top candidate from the leading selected b-jet and the hadronic W candidate."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic top candidate from the leading b-jet in the event and the hadronic W candidate."
