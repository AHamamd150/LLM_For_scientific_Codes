[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic top candidate from the leading b-jet and the hadronic W candidate."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether "leading b-jet" refers to the highest-pT b-jet among all jets in the event (before any selection), or the highest-pT b-jet among the selected b-jets (after cuts and b-tagging).

Question to user:
Should the "leading b-jet" be defined as the highest-pT b-jet in the full event (before any cuts), or among the selected b-jets (after b-tagging and pT/eta cuts)?

Golden axe:
Leading b-jet is the highest-pT b-jet among the selected b-jets (after b-tagging and pT/eta cuts).

Silver axe:
Leading b-jet is the highest-pT b-jet in the full event, regardless of selection status.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic top candidate from the leading selected b-jet and the hadronic W candidate."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic top candidate from the leading b-jet in the event (regardless of selection) and the hadronic W candidate."

[AMBIGUITY 2] Non-b-tagged jet selection

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets."

Why this is dangerous:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether "non-b-tagged jets" refers to jets that are explicitly not b-tagged (i.e., light jets), or simply jets that are not among the selected b-jets (which may include jets that are not b-tagged but also not selected due to pT/eta cuts).

Question to user:
Should "non-b-tagged jets" be interpreted as jets that are explicitly not b-tagged (regardless of selection status), or as jets that are not among the selected b-jets (i.e., light jets that passed pT/eta cuts)?

Golden axe:
"Non-b-tagged jets" refers to jets that are not b-tagged and passed pT/eta cuts (i.e., selected light jets).

"Non-b-tagged jets" refers to any jets in the event that are not b-tagged, regardless of whether they passed pT/eta cuts.

Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected non-b-tagged jets."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets in the event (regardless of selection status)."
