[AMBIGUITY 1] Leading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic top candidate from the leading b-jet and the hadronic W candidate."

Why this is dangerous:
The phrase "leading b-jet" could refer to the highest-pT b-tagged jet in the full event, or only among the selected b-jets after earlier cuts. This affects which jet is used in the top candidate construction and validation plots.

Question to user:
Should the "leading b-jet" be defined as the highest-pT b-tagged jet in the full event, or only among the b-jets that passed all previous selection criteria?

Golden axe:
The leading b-jet is the highest-pT b-tagged jet in the full event, regardless of earlier cuts.

Silver axe:
The leading b-jet is the highest-pT b-tagged jet among those that passed all previous selection criteria.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

POSSIBLE_CLARIFYING_REWRITES:
- Golden axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic top candidate from the highest-pT b-tagged jet in the event and the hadronic W candidate."
- Silver axe rewrite: "Build the hadronic top candidate from the leading b-jet (after all previous cuts) and the hadronic W candidate."
