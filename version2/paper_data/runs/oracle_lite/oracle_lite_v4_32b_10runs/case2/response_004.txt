[CANDIDATE 1] Lepton collection consistency

Original phrase:
"Require exactly one lepton (electron or muon)"

Unresolved choice:
Does "lepton" refer to the raw collection of electrons and muons, or to those already passing the pT and |eta| cuts?

Why this may matter:
If the lepton count is applied before or after the pT/eta cuts, it could significantly change the event selection. The helper_get_leptons function returns uncut leptons, while the cuts on electrons and muons may have already been applied.

[CANDIDATE 2] Jet collection consistency

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets."

Unresolved choice:
Are these jets selected before or after the "at least 4 jets" and "at least 2 b-tagged jets" cuts?

Why this may matter:
If the jet selection for the hadronic W candidate is applied before or after the other jet-related cuts, it could change which jets are available for building the W candidate, affecting the invariant mass calculation.

[CANDIDATE 3] B-jet collection consistency

Original phrase:

Unresolved choice:

Why this may matter:
If the b-jet selection for the top candidate is applied before or after additional cuts, it could change which b-jet is used for the top candidate, affecting the invariant mass calculation.
