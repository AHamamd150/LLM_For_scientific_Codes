[CANDIDATE 1] Leading and subleading tau definition

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Unresolved choice:
It is unclear whether the leading and subleading tau are defined based on pT of the selected taus, and whether any additional cuts (e.g., isolation, decay mode) are applied before ranking.

Why this may matter:
If the selection of leading and subleading tau is not based on pT, or if the ranking is done before or after additional cuts, the resulting ditau system could change, affecting invariant mass and angular distributions.
