[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Dijet invariant mass selection refers to uncut or cut jets

Original phrase:
"Select invariant mass of the leading and subleading jets (m_jj) > 400 GeV"

Why this matters:
This phrase does not specify whether the jets used to compute m_jj are the raw jets in the event or the jets that have already passed the pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 4.7 cuts. Using uncut jets could include low-pT or out-of-eta jets in the dijet mass calculation, which would lead to a different distribution than using only selected jets.

Question to user:
Should the dijet invariant mass be computed from the jets that have already passed the pT and eta cuts, or from the full jet collection in the event?

Golden axe:
Use jets that have passed the pT and eta cuts.

Silver axe:
Use all jets in the event, regardless of whether they pass the pT and eta cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
