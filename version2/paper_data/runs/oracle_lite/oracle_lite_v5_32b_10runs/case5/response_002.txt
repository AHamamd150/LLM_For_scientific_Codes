[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Dijet invariant mass selection scope

Original phrase:
"Select invariant mass of the leading and subleading jets (m_jj) > 400 GeV"

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether this cut applies to the selected jets (after pT and eta cuts) or to all jets in the event. This affects which jets are considered in the dijet system.

Question to user:
Should the m_jj > 400 GeV cut be computed from the selected jets (after pT and eta cuts) or from all jets in the event?

Golden axe:
Compute m_jj from the selected jets (after pT and eta cuts).

Silver axe:
Compute m_jj from all jets in the event, regardless of selection.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

Golden axe
