[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Ambiguity in "leading tau and subleading tau" definition

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether the leading and subleading taus are determined before or after the "opposite-sign tau pair" cut. This affects which pair of taus is used to build the ditau system.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading taus defined before or after requiring the tau pair to be opposite-sign?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading are defined from the selected taus before the opposite-sign requirement.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading are defined from the selected taus after the opposite-sign requirement is applied.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
