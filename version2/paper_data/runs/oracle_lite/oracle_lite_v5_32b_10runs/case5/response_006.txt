[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Definition of "leading tau" and "subleading tau" in context of cuts

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether the "leading" and "subleading" taus are defined before or after the "opposite-sign tau pair" cut. If the cut is applied before defining the pair, the leading and subleading may not be the correct ones for the OS requirement. If the cut is applied after, the selection of leading and subleading may change based on the OS condition.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading taus defined before or after the opposite-sign requirement is applied?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined from the selected taus before the opposite-sign cut.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading taus are defined only after ensuring the pair is opposite-sign.

Recommended default: Golden axe
