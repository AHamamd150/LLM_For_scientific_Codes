[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Ambiguity in "leading tau and subleading tau" definition

Original phrase:
"Define the leading tau and subleading tau from the selected taus."

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether the leading and subleading taus are defined before or after the "opposite-sign tau pair" cut. This affects whether the selection of the leading/subleading pair is based on all selected taus or only on the surviving opposite-sign pair.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading taus defined from all selected taus (before the opposite-sign requirement), or only from the subset of taus that form an opposite-sign pair?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading are defined from all selected taus, before applying the opposite-sign requirement.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading are defined from the opposite-sign pair only, after the requirement is applied.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

Original phrase:

Question to user:
Are the ditau and dijet systems used in the delta phi plot the same as those defined earlier (from leading/subleading taus and jets), or are they redefined for this purpose?

Golden axe:
The ditau and dijet systems are the same as those defined earlier in the selection.

Silver axe:
The ditau and dijet systems are redefined for the purpose of the delta phi plot.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
