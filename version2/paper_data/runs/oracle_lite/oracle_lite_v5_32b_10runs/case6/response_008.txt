[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Definition of "selected b-tagged jets" for delta R computation

Original phrase:
"Among the selected b-tagged jets, define the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R."

Why this matters:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether "selected b-tagged jets" refers to all jets that are both b-tagged and pass the jet pT/eta cuts, or only those that survive all previous selection steps (including the lepton+jets multiplicity and MET cuts). The delta R computation could yield different results depending on which collection is used.

Question to user:
Are the "selected b-tagged jets" for the delta R computation defined before or after the MET and W mass cuts?

Golden axe:
Use all b-tagged jets that pass the jet pT/eta cuts and the b-tagging requirement.

Silver axe:
Use only b-tagged jets that survive all previous selection steps including the MET and W mass cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[UNDERSPECIFICATION 2] Definition of "leading" and "subleading" b-jets

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet" and "Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet"

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether the leading and subleading b-jets are defined before or after the delta R selection ("b-jet closest to the selected lepton"). This affects which jets are considered in the ordering.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading b-jets defined before or after selecting the b-jet closest to the lepton in delta R?

Golden axe:
Define leading and subleading b-jets among all selected b-tagged jets before applying the delta R selection.

Silver axe:
Define leading and subleading b-jets only among the b-tagged jets that are not the one closest to the lepton.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[UNDERSPECIFICATION 3] Definition of "selected non-b-tagged jets" for hadronic W

Original phrase:

This affects which jets are available for building the hadronic W.

Question to user:

Golden axe:
Use all non-b-tagged jets that pass the jet pT/eta cuts.

Silver axe:
Use only non-b-tagged jets that survive all previous selection steps including the MET and W mass cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
