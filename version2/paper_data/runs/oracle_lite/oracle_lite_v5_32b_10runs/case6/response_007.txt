[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Definition of "selected b-tagged jets" for delta R computation

Original phrase:
"Among the selected b-tagged jets, define the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R."

Why this matters:
This phrase is ambiguous about whether "selected b-tagged jets" refers to the jets that passed the earlier cuts (pT > 30 GeV, |eta| < 2.5, and b-tagged) or to a further subset of jets that may have been modified by later cuts (e.g., after the hadronic W candidate is built). This affects which jets are considered in the delta R calculation.

Question to user:
Are the "selected b-tagged jets" for the delta R computation the ones that passed the initial jet and b-tagging cuts, or a subset that may have been further filtered in later steps?

Golden axe:
Use the b-tagged jets that passed the initial jet and b-tagging cuts.

Silver axe:
Use only the b-tagged jets that remain after all prior cuts, including the hadronic W candidate selection.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
