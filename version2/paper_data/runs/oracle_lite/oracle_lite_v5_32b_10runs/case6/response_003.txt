[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Lepton selection and definition of "exactly one lepton"

Original phrase:
"Require exactly one lepton (electron or muon)"

Why this matters:
This phrase does not specify whether the lepton must be selected after applying the pT and |eta| cuts, or whether it refers to the raw lepton collection. This choice affects whether the "exactly one" requirement is applied before or after the lepton cuts.

Question to user:
Are we to require exactly one lepton from the raw lepton collection, or from the lepton collection after applying the pT and |eta| cuts?

Golden axe:
Exactly one lepton is required from the lepton collection after applying the pT and |eta| cuts.

Silver axe:
Exactly one lepton is required from the raw lepton collection, before applying the pT and |eta| cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[UNDERSPECIFICATION 2] Definition of "selected b-tagged jets" for the closest b-jet to the lepton

Original phrase:
"Among the selected b-tagged jets, define the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R."

Why this matters:
It is unclear whether "selected b-tagged jets" refers to the full collection of b-tagged jets in the event, or only to those that have passed all prior cuts (e.g., pT and |eta| cuts on jets, and the b-tagging requirement). This choice affects which jets are considered in the delta R calculation.

Question to user:
Are we to consider all b-tagged jets in the event, or only those that have passed the jet pT and |eta| cuts, when finding the b-jet closest to the selected lepton?

Only b-tagged jets that have passed the jet pT and |eta| cuts are considered.

Silver axe:
All b-tagged jets in the event are considered, regardless of whether they passed the jet pT and |eta| cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
