[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] b-jet selection for delta R with lepton

Original phrase:
"Among these selected jets, require at least 2 b-tagged jets. Among the selected b-tagged jets, define the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R."

Why this matters:
The phrase is ambiguous about whether the b-jet closest to the lepton is selected from all b-tagged jets in the event or only from those that passed the earlier jet selection (pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5). This affects which jets are considered in the delta R calculation and could change the resulting object.

Question to user:
Are the b-tagged jets used to find the closest one to the lepton required to have passed the jet pT and eta cuts, or are all b-tagged jets in the event considered regardless of those cuts?

Golden axe:
Use only the b-tagged jets that passed the jet pT and eta cuts.

Silver axe:
Use all b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of jet pT and eta cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[UNDERSPECIFICATION 2] Leading and subleading b-jet definition

Original phrase:
"Plot the pT of the leading b-jet" and "Plot the pT of the subleading b-jet"

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether the leading and subleading b-jets are defined based on the selected b-tagged jets (those that passed the jet pT and eta cuts), or based on all b-tagged jets in the event. This choice affects which jets are considered in the plots.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading b-jets defined from all b-tagged jets in the event, or only from the subset that passed the jet pT and eta cuts?

Golden axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are defined from the subset of b-tagged jets that passed the jet pT and eta cuts.

Silver axe:
Leading and subleading b-jets are defined from all b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of jet pT and eta cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[UNDERSPECIFICATION 3] Hadronic W candidate construction

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading selected non-b-tagged jets."

Why this matters:
The phrase is ambiguous about whether "selected non-b-tagged jets" refers to jets that passed the jet pT and eta cuts but are not b-tagged, or whether it refers to a separate selection of non-b-tagged jets. This affects which jets are used to build the hadronic W candidate.

Question to user:
Are the two leading jets used to build the hadronic W candidate selected from all non-b-tagged jets in the event, or only from the subset of jets that passed the jet pT and eta cuts?

Golden axe:
Use the two leading jets from the subset of jets that passed the jet pT and eta cuts and are not b-tagged.

Silver axe:
Use the two leading jets from all non-b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of jet pT and eta cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
