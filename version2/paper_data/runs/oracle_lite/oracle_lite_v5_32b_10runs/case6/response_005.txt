[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Definition of "selected b-tagged jets" for delta R computation

Original phrase:
"Among the selected b-tagged jets, define the b-jet closest to the selected lepton in delta R."

Why this matters:
The phrase "selected b-tagged jets" could refer to the full collection of b-tagged jets in the event (after pT and eta cuts), or it could refer to a subset of jets that survived earlier cuts (e.g. after the jet multiplicity and b-tag multiplicity cuts). This affects which jets are considered when computing the delta R to the lepton.

Question to user:
Are we computing the delta R between the lepton and the closest b-tagged jet among all jets in the event (after pT/eta cuts), or only among jets that passed all previous selection cuts (including jet multiplicity and b-tag multiplicity)?

Golden axe:
Compute delta R using all b-tagged jets in the event that passed pT and eta cuts.

Silver axe:
Compute delta R using only b-tagged jets that survived all previous selection cuts.

Recommended default: Golden axe
