[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Hadronic W candidate jet selection timing

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets."

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether "leading non-b-tagged jets" refers to the two highest-pT jets among all non-b-tagged jets in the event, or whether it refers to the two highest-pT jets after applying the jet pT and eta cuts. This affects which jets are used for the hadronic W candidate and thus the invariant mass calculation.

Question to user:
Are the two leading non-b-tagged jets used for the hadronic W candidate selected before or after applying the jet pT and eta cuts?

Golden axe:
Use the two highest-pT non-b-tagged jets that pass the jet pT and eta cuts.

Silver axe:
Use the two highest-pT non-b-tagged jets in the event, regardless of whether they pass the jet pT and eta cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[UNDERSPECIFICATION 2] Lepton definition for plots

Original phrase:
"Plot the delta R between the leading lepton and the closest b-jet"

Why this matters:
The phrase does not clarify whether "leading lepton" refers to the highest-pT lepton among all selected leptons (after the lepton pT and eta cuts), or whether it refers to the highest-pT lepton in the event regardless of whether it passes the lepton cuts. This affects which lepton is used for the delta R calculation.

Question to user:
Is the "leading lepton" in the delta R plot the highest-pT lepton that passes the lepton pT and eta cuts, or the highest-pT lepton in the event regardless of cuts?

Golden axe:
Use the highest-pT lepton that passes the lepton pT and eta cuts.

Silver axe:
Use the highest-pT lepton in the event, regardless of whether it passes the lepton cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
