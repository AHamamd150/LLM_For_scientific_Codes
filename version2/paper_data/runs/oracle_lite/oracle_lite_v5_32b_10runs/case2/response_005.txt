[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Hadronic W candidate jet selection timing

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets."

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether the "leading non-b-tagged jets" are determined before or after the "at least 2 b-tagged jets" cut. This affects which jets are considered for the hadronic W candidate.

Question to user:
Are the two leading non-b-tagged jets used for the hadronic W candidate selected before or after the requirement of at least 2 b-tagged jets?

Golden axe:
Select leading non-b-tagged jets after the b-jet count requirement.

Silver axe:
Select leading non-b-tagged jets before the b-jet count requirement.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[UNDERSPECIFICATION 2] Lepton definition for plots

Original phrase:
"Plot the delta R between the leading lepton and the closest b-jet"

Why this matters:

Does "leading lepton" in the delta R plot refer to the single selected lepton in the event, or is it a specific lepton from a hypothesized decay chain?

Golden axe:
Use the single selected lepton in the event.

Silver axe:
Use a lepton from a specific decay hypothesis (e.g., from top decay).

Recommended default:
Golden axe
