[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Hadronic W candidate jet selection timing

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets."

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether "non-b-tagged jets" refers to all jets in the event or only to a subset of jets that have already passed earlier selection criteria (e.g., pT > 30 GeV, |eta| < 2.5). This affects which jets are considered for building the hadronic W candidate.

Question to user:
Should the two leading non-b-tagged jets used to build the hadronic W candidate be selected from all jets in the event, or only from jets that have already passed the pT and eta cuts?

Golden axe:
Use jets that have already passed the pT and eta cuts.

Silver axe:
Use all jets in the event, regardless of whether they passed earlier cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
