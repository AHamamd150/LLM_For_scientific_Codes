Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets."

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether "leading non-b-tagged jets" refers to the two highest-pT jets among all non-b-tagged jets in the event, or among non-b-tagged jets that have already passed the jet pT and eta cuts. This affects which jets are used for the hadronic W candidate and could change the invariant mass distribution.

Question to user:
Are the two leading non-b-tagged jets used for the hadronic W candidate selected from all non-b-tagged jets in the event, or only from those that have passed the jet pT and eta cuts?

Golden axe:
Select the two leading non-b-tagged jets from all non-b-tagged jets in the event.

Silver axe:
Select the two leading non-b-tagged jets from non-b-tagged jets that have passed the jet pT and eta cuts.

Golden axe
