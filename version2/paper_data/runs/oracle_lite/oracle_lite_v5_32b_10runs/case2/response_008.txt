[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Hadronic W candidate jet selection timing

Original phrase:
"Build the hadronic W candidate from the two leading non-b-tagged jets."

Why this matters:
The phrase does not specify whether the jets are selected before or after the "at least 2 b-tagged jets" cut. This affects whether the non-b-tagged jets are chosen from the full jet collection or only from the remaining jets after b-tag selection.

Question to user:
Are the two leading non-b-tagged jets used for the hadronic W candidate selected before or after the requirement of at least 2 b-tagged jets?

Golden axe:
Select the two leading non-b-tagged jets from the full jet collection before applying the b-tag count requirement.

Silver axe:
Select the two leading non-b-tagged jets only from the jets that remain after the b-tag count requirement has been applied.

Recommended default: Golden axe
