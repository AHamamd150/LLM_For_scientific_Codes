[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Ambiguity in "selected jets" for b-tag veto

Original phrase:
"Veto events with b-tagged jets among the selected jets"

Why this matters:
The phrase "selected jets" could refer to jets that passed the pT and |eta| cuts, or to jets that passed all previous cuts including the jet multiplicity and jet selection. This ambiguity affects whether the veto applies to all jets in the event or only to a subset that has already been filtered.

Question to user:
Does the b-tag veto apply to all jets in the event that passed the pT and |eta| cuts, or only to the jets that have already been selected for the dijet system (i.e., the leading and subleading jets)?

Golden axe:
Apply the veto to all jets that passed the pT and |eta| cuts.

Silver axe:
Apply the veto only to the jets that are used to define the dijet system.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
