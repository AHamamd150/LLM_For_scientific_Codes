"Veto events with b-tagged jets among the selected jets"

The b-tag veto could apply to all jets in the event or only to the jets used in the dijet system.

Question to user:
Should the b-tag veto apply to all jets in the event that pass the basic jet cuts (pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 4.7), or only to the jets used in the dijet system (i.e., the leading and subleading jets)?

Golden axe:

Silver axe:
Apply the veto only to the leading and subleading jets used in the dijet system.
