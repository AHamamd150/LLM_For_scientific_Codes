[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Ambiguity in "selected jets" for b-tag veto

Original phrase:
"Veto events with b-tagged jets among the selected jets"

Why this matters:
The phrase "selected jets" is not explicitly defined in the input. It could refer to jets that passed the pT and |eta| cuts (30 GeV and 4.7), or it could refer to a further subset (e.g., jets used in the dijet system). The b-tag veto could thus apply to all jets in the event, only the jets used in the dijet system, or a different subset.

Question to user:
Do you want the b-tag veto to apply to all jets that passed the pT and |eta| cuts, or only to the jets used in the dijet system (i.e., the leading and subleading jets)?

Golden axe:
Apply the veto to all jets that passed the pT and |eta| cuts.

Silver axe:
Apply the veto only to the leading and subleading jets used in the dijet system.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
