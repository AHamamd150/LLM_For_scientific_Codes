[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Diphoton system reuse in pT and mass

Original phrase:
"Build the diphoton system from the leading photon and the subleading photon. [...] Use this same diphoton system to compute diphoton pT."

Why this matters:
The phrase implies a diphoton system is built once and reused for both invariant mass and pT. However, it is not clear whether the diphoton pT is computed from the same system or if a new system is built for pT. This could lead to different results if, for example, the pT is computed from the same four-momentum sum as the mass or from a different method.

Question to user:
Are the diphoton pT and invariant mass computed from the same diphoton system (i.e., the same pair of photons and their four-momenta)?

Golden axe:
Compute both diphoton pT and invariant mass from the same diphoton system built from the leading and subleading photons.

Silver axe:
Compute diphoton invariant mass from the system, but compute diphoton pT using a different method or from a different system.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

[UNDERSPECIFICATION 2] Leading and subleading photon definition timing

Original phrase:
"Define the leading photon and subleading photon after photon selection. [...] Leading photon pT > 35 GeV [...] Subleading photon pT > 25 GeV"

Why this matters:
It is not clear whether the leading and subleading photons are defined *after* the pT cuts (35 GeV and 25 GeV) or *before*. This affects which photons are considered for the roles of "leading" and "subleading" and may change the final selection.

Question to user:
Are the leading and subleading photons determined before or after applying the pT cuts of 35 GeV and 25 GeV?

Golden axe:
Define leading and subleading photons after applying the pT cuts.

Silver axe:
Define leading and subleading photons before applying the pT cuts.

Recommended default:
Golden axe
