[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Leading jets definition in dijet invariant mass cut

Original phrase:
"Select invariant mass of the two leading jets between 60 and 100 GeV (W hadronic candidate)"

Why this matters:
The phrase "leading jets" could refer to the two highest-pT jets in the full event or in the selected jet collection. This choice affects which jets are used for the invariant mass calculation and thus the event selection.

Question to user:
Are the "leading jets" for the dijet invariant mass cut selected from all jets in the event or only from the jets passing the pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5 selection?

Golden axe:
Leading jets are selected from the jets passing the pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5 selection.

Silver axe:
Leading jets are selected from all jets in the event, regardless of whether they pass the pT and |eta| cuts.
