[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Lepton definition for plots and outputs

Original phrase:
"Use selected electrons and selected muons to define selected leptons."

Why this matters:
The phrase "selected leptons" is defined as the union of selected electrons and selected muons, but it is unclear whether this definition is meant to be used consistently for all subsequent plots and outputs, or if the term "lepton" later refers to the specific type (electron or muon) that passed the selection. This could affect plots like "pT of the lepton" or "eta of the lepton" if the user expects to treat electrons and muons separately.

Question to user:
Should all plots and outputs that refer to "the lepton" treat it as a single merged collection (electron or muon), or should they be split into electron and muon channels?

Golden axe:
Treat "the lepton" as a single merged collection of selected electrons and muons.

Silver axe:
Treat "the lepton" as separate channels (electron or muon) depending on which passed the selection.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

Golden axe
