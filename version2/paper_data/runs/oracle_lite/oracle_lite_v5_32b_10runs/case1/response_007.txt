[UNDERSPECIFICATION 1] Definition of "selected jets" for dijet invariant mass and veto

Original phrase:
"Require at least 2 jets with pT > 30 GeV and |eta| < 2.5"
"Veto events with b-tagged jets"
"Select invariant mass of the two leading jets between 60 and 100 GeV (W hadronic candidate)"

Why this matters:
The phrase "b-tagged jets" could refer to jets from the full event or only to the selected jets (pT > 30 GeV, |eta| < 2.5). Similarly, the two leading jets for the invariant mass could be from the full jet collection or only from the selected jets. This choice affects which jets are considered for vetoing and for building the W hadronic candidate.

Question to user:
Are the b-tagged jets to be vetoed from the full event, or only from the selected jets (pT > 30 GeV, |eta| < 2.5)? Are the two leading jets for the invariant mass to be selected from the full jet collection or only from the selected jets?

Golden axe:
Apply the b-tag veto and leading jet selection to the selected jets (pT > 30 GeV, |eta| < 2.5).

Silver axe:
Apply the b-tag veto to all jets in the event, and select the leading jets from the full jet collection.

Recommended default:
Golden axe

"Select invariant mass of the two leading jets between 60 and 100 GeV (W hadronic candidate)"

If defined after, the leading jets are from the selected jets only. This affects the jet pair used for both the delta R and invariant mass cuts.

Define the two leading jets from the selected jets (pT > 30 GeV, |eta| < 2.5).

Silver axe:

Golden axe
