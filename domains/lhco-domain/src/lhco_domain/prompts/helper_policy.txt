The spec is an LHCO physics-analysis specification.

Every explicit cut, veto, reconstruction step, plotted quantity, histogram,
requested observable, and requested output quantity stated in the spec should
be accounted for by the selected helpers.

Select domain-specific helpers only when the spec explicitly names the
corresponding physics object, decay channel, reconstruction target, or
observable.

Do not select helpers from other physics channels or reconstruction targets
merely because they involve similar particles, masses, or kinematic variables.

If the spec first defines selected physics objects, prefer helpers whose
semantics match that selected-object workflow for downstream cuts, observables,
plots, histograms, or output quantities.
